You are right to distrust a tool that handles videos of people. No need to believe us: everything can be checked.

What Anonymizer does

  • On your device. The video is analysed in the browser. It is sent nowhere.
  • A solid black square. Painted on the frame before it is saved again: the face is not blurred or covered, it is no longer in the file.
  • No traces. Location, date and phone model, often kept in the file, are removed.
  • No account. No ads, no analytics. Like any site, the host (Cloudflare) sees who connects to anonymizer.nvp.qzz.io, never the videos.

If you can code

  • Public code. Free licence: github.com/yakuzaax/anonymizer. The site serves the same readable files, nothing hidden.
  • No trace of the face. test/test-no-trace.mjs takes two videos identical except for the faces and applies the same masks: the output files are byte-for-byte identical. No watermark can lead back to the face.
  • Nothing sent. The page forbids the browser from connecting to any site other than anonymizer.nvp.qzz.io.
  • Original libraries. ONNX Runtime and Mediabunny: test/verify-vendor.sh checks they match the official releases. The detection model is YuNet, retrained to see better at night: the recipe is public.

What Anonymizer does not do

No tool finds every face: a profile, a partly hidden or very small face can be missed. Tap the image to add a mask by hand.

The square hides the face, not clothes, tattoos, gait, place or voice. Sound is kept by default: turn it off with the “Sound” button.
